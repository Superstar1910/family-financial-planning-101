# Fictional case study: the Nsubuga family

Sarah earns UGX 2,200,000 per month. Daniel's repair business varies, and the household budgets only UGX 1,000,000 of his expected monthly contribution. Their conservative planning income is UGX 3,200,000.

Before changes, essentials and obligations are UGX 2,540,000, debt service is UGX 310,000 and unplanned/discretionary spending is UGX 420,000. Total outflow is UGX 3,270,000, producing a UGX 70,000 shortfall.

They reduce essentials and obligations to UGX 2,420,000 through bill review and planning, keep debt service at UGX 310,000 and choose a UGX 220,000 enjoyment/discretionary limit. Total outflow becomes UGX 2,950,000, producing a UGX 250,000 difference.

They allocate UGX 150,000 to school fees, UGX 75,000 to an emergency reserve and UGX 25,000 to a long-term home goal. All figures are invented and are not national averages.
