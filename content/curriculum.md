# Curriculum map

## Learning outcomes

Readers should be able to describe household cash flow; identify a practical surplus or shortfall; prioritise goals; build a protection plan; compare debt; distinguish saving from investing; understand risk, liquidity, diversification, inflation and fees; verify providers; and run a monthly family money meeting.

## Paths

- **5 minutes:** diagnostic, framework and next move
- **30 minutes:** seven-move core lesson and one calculator
- **Working session:** case study, worksheets, family meeting and 30-day plan

## Teaching order

Purpose → snapshot → spending → earning → surplus → protection → debt → goal buckets → investing → family system → action.

## Facilitation notes

Use adult-to-adult language. Invite participants to use invented or rounded numbers in group settings. Never require public disclosure of debt, income, family conflict or account details. Distinguish examples from rules and pause for jurisdiction-specific verification.
