# Glossary

- **Asset:** Something the household owns or controls that has economic value.
- **Beneficiary:** A person or entity designated to receive a benefit or asset, subject to applicable rules and law.
- **Cash flow:** Money entering and leaving the household over a period.
- **Compounding:** Growth earned on earlier growth as well as the original amount.
- **Diversification:** Spreading exposure so one loss does not determine the whole outcome.
- **Emergency reserve:** Accessible money set aside for material, unexpected household needs.
- **Inflation:** A broad rise in prices that reduces what money can buy.
- **Liquidity:** How quickly an asset can become spendable cash without a large loss.
- **Nominal return:** Return before adjusting for inflation.
- **Real return:** Return after inflation; fees and taxes may reduce it further.
- **Sinking fund:** Money accumulated gradually for a known future cost.
