# Editorial and responsible-communications policy

Write in plain adult English. Define technical terms on first use. Acknowledge irregular income, extended-family responsibilities and different household structures without stereotyping or shame.

Separate fact, assumption, illustration and opinion. Do not promise returns, imply capital protection, disguise marketing as education or recommend unverified providers. Explain uncertainty, liquidity, fees, inflation, concentration and the possibility of loss.

Material current claims require direct support from a primary or authoritative source and an “as of” date. Corrections should be visible in the changelog. General education must not be framed as a substitute for personalised professional advice.
