# Localisation guide

Localisation requires more than changing currency symbols.

1. Confirm the target jurisdiction, languages and audience realities.
2. Replace currency formatting in `calculators.js` and examples.
3. Verify regulators for banking/payments, capital markets, pensions and insurance.
4. Review local borrowing conventions, pension structures, deposit protection, consumer recourse, tax treatment and estate/beneficiary rules.
5. Replace regulator links only after checking official domains.
6. Adapt names and examples respectfully without presenting them as averages.
7. Update the source register, review date, disclaimer and QA record.

Never transplant a rule, tax rate, licence category or product assumption across countries without primary-source verification.
