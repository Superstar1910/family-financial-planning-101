# Methodology

The pack uses a behaviour-first learning sequence: purpose, visibility, cash-flow choices, income resilience, protection, debt, goal-based saving/investing and a repeatable family review routine.

## Calculation conventions

- Currency values are rounded to whole Uganda shillings.
- The goal calculator excludes returns, inflation, tax and fees.
- Compound growth assumes monthly compounding and end-of-month contributions at a constant illustrative nominal rate. Actual returns vary and may be negative.
- The debt illustration uses a standard reducing-balance amortisation formula with equal monthly payments. It excludes fees, taxes, insurance, penalties and variable-rate changes.
- Emergency-reserve outputs are educational ranges based on user-selected income stability, not prescriptions.

## Evidence boundary

The fictional household numbers are internally consistent examples, not claims about typical Ugandan families. Regulatory references are maintained in the source register. The publication does not rank or recommend products.
