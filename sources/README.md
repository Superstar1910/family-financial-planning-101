# Sources and evidence

The public pack deliberately avoids volatile rates, tax thresholds, return assumptions presented as expectations, market-size statistics and product comparisons. Current jurisdiction-specific guidance is limited to identifying relevant Ugandan regulators and advising readers to verify providers and rules directly.

`source-register.csv` records the claim or use, institution, title, URL, dates, jurisdiction, verification status and review date. “Verified” means the source was accessed and directly supported the stated use on the access date; it does not guarantee that the page or rule remains unchanged.

## Review rules

- Prefer primary regulators, legislation, statistics agencies and recognised multilateral sources.
- Date every current claim.
- Do not cite search-result pages.
- Never convert a fictional illustration into a national fact.
- Do not silently revise historical figures; record changes in the changelog.
- Recheck live licence/provider registers at the point of a material decision.
